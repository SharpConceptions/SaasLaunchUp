SALES GUIDE

Call script

Draft guide. Use your approved disclosures and adapt the wording to the contact and campaign.

1. Open

“Hi, is this [name]? This is [your name] with [company]. Is now a good time for a brief conversation?”

2. Reason for calling

“I see your company does: [research the company website first]. I’d like to learn whether this is relevant to your team.”

3. Discover

  • “How are you handling [topic] today?”
  • “What is working well, and what would you change?”
  • “Who else is involved in deciding next steps?”

4. Recap and next step

“What I heard is [summary]. Would it be useful to schedule [specific next step]?” Confirm the time and preferred follow-up channel.

If they decline or opt out

Thank them, stop the pitch, and record the opt-out or Do Not Call request immediately.
