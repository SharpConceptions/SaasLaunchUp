# SaaS Launchup CRM

The workspace places its main sections and the active section's pages in one compact left sidebar. The top bar keeps Admin center and profile controls. A fixed footer reads “SaaS Launchup™ · © 2026 SCDA LLC. All rights reserved.”

The profile button opens Settings, with Reporting in its sidebar. Sales reports remain available to owners and sales managers. Marketing contains Social Media. Customer service contains the existing review previews plus saved customer tickets, billing issues, and internal bug reports. Owners and sales managers can create and update tickets; support read-only users can review them. Billing overview shows manually recorded purchases for owners. Payment provider data, invoices, social publishing, and technical team notifications require future integrations.

Customer service also has cancellation requests with a customer, account or subscription reference, requested date, reason, and review status. Closing the request does not cancel a plan or stop billing. Sales has an Inbound calls page where owners and sales managers can save business hours, queue or voicemail routing preferences, and greeting text. Sales representatives can review those settings. Incoming call handling, a live number, and voicemail require a Twilio voice connection and are currently unavailable.

Communications now appears within Sales and Marketing instead of a separate top bar area. Sales includes SMS, Calls, text templates, an SMS inbox, and consent status. Marketing includes email campaigns, email templates, an email inbox, and email consent. Templates are saved per company and channel. A marketing email draft can start from a saved email template. Automation drafts can reference text templates, email templates, or saved email campaign drafts, and define form signup, ad lead, or awareness engagement triggers in addition to pipeline events. Sales call task actions can also be drafted. Automation rules keep a snapshot of the selected message and remain paused. Form and ad event capture, analytics signals, consent enforcement, and delivery providers must be connected before an automation can run or a message can be sent.

The Sales sidebar now uses one Calls entry. Its in-page bar switches between the Sales dialer, Inbound calls, SMS, and SMS inbox while keeping Calls selected in the sidebar. Call queues, recordings and notes, text templates, and consent are available as shortcuts within the relevant call or text view.

This repository contains the SaaS Launchup private Sites application. `/` opens the workspace. Business owners land on the Dashboard; sales roles land in Sales. `/foundation` is the persistent CRM foundation backed by Cloudflare D1.

The foundation includes organizations, contacts, companies, tasks, pipelines, notes, membership checks, and an append-only audit trail. Database schema and initial migration are in `db/schema.ts` and `drizzle/`. The Sales Dialer demonstration includes a collapsible number pad and movable call script; calls remain disabled until Twilio is connected.

Contacts can be moved between pipeline stages by selecting a contact card. The saved CRM records stage changes in the audit trail. Company research reads a public HTTPS website description, stores the source and summary on a CRM company record, and makes that wording available in the Sales Dialer call script for the rep to review.

Sales Pipeline supports multiple named pipelines, stage creation and renaming, stage order, and assigning saved contacts to a pipeline. Pipeline and stage IDs are stored on contacts so stages with the same name in different pipelines remain distinct. Business owners and sales managers can configure pipelines. The Pipeline page links to Funnels & Automation → Automations, where managers can save draft rules for contact or opportunity stage entry and won or lost outcomes. Draft actions include email, SMS, and public HTTPS webhooks. Rules do not execute until provider connections, consent and suppression checks, credential handling, and delivery controls are added.

The Sales contact panel lets a salesperson save notes on a contact and turn a note into a follow-up task assigned to their own profile. My dashboard lists their notes and open tasks, and the Tasks view lists tasks created from notes. Saved CRM contacts use D1 and associate notes and tasks with the authenticated user. The sample contacts use clearly labeled browser-local demo notes and tasks.

Marketing has five sections: Email marketing for campaign drafts, Idea board for draggable idea and action cards, Media studio for branded text graphics with PNG download, SEO · GEO · AEO scan for checking public page markup, and Blog studio for article drafts and search metadata. Drafts, boards, and scan reports are saved in D1 for the company. Email sending and blog publishing remain disabled until their providers and consent controls are connected. The scan checks HTML metadata and schema only; it does not measure rankings or AI citations.

The admin Dashboard has Overview, Pipeline, Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, Experience, and Loyalty views. Pipeline metrics use saved opportunities and CRM contacts. Owners can add and edit opportunities, record purchases against a unique verified order reference, record customer check-ins, and update manually recorded subscription status. MRR uses active monthly amounts from these records. The dashboard never counts a won deal as a verified payment. Ads, social, GA4, heatmaps, automated payment, and SaaS product usage metrics show unavailable states until their providers are connected. Sales representatives only see Sales navigation, and sales managers can also see Sales reporting.

Run `npm ci` and `npm run build`. After schema changes, regenerate the migration. The hosted Site is owner-private. Customer identity and invitations are not yet implemented. Do not onboard external companies until that access model is complete.

See `../docs/FOUNDATION_AND_INTEGRATIONS.md` for the rollout and provider requirements.
